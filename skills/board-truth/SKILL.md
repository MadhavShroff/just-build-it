# Keep the board true

The board is the shared record of work. A wrong card costs more than a missing card.

## Rules
1. Mark a task done only when you have evidence: a receipt, a commit, and a passing command.
2. Write the evidence on the card: the command, the exit code and the commit SHA.
3. Mark a task failed or blocked as soon as you know. Write the reason.
4. Update the card before you start a step and after you finish it.
5. Never move a card for work another agent owns.

## Decisions
Open a decision when the answer needs human authority: scope, spending, priorities, or an action that cannot be undone.

1. Write the question in one sentence.
2. Give two to four options. Mark your recommendation.
3. State what stays blocked until the answer arrives.
4. Do not wait idle. Continue any task that does not depend on the answer.

## Do not
- Do not ask a decision for work that you can decide and record as an assumption.
- Do not close a decision yourself.
